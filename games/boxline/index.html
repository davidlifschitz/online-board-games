Boxline

Claim one edge at a time. Close a box to score it and keep the turn. The quiet moves decide who inherits the chains.

How to play

Tap a horizontal or vertical gap. Completing one or two boxes scores them and gives another move. When all edges are claimed, the highest score wins.
