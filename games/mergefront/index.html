Mergefront

Slide equal tiles together. Race on the same seed, share one board, or pressure an opponent with blocker tiles.

ModeSolo
Target2048
Clock—
Replay0 moves

Controls

Player 1: W A S D. Player 2: arrow keys. On touch devices, swipe directly on a board. In co-op, each successful move hands control to the other player.

In Battle, a merge of 64 or higher sends one to three blockers to the other board. Blockers split a row or column into independent sliding segments.

Rules & provenance

The engine is an original deterministic implementation. The classic slide/merge semantics were audited against permissively licensed 2048 implementations; no upstream artwork or branding is used. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the repository.
