How to play

1. Draft. Take every tile of one color from one workshop, moving its other tiles to the center, or take a color directly from the center.

2. Build a line. Put the drafted tiles into one pattern line. A line can contain only one color, and you cannot prepare a color already present in that wall row.

3. Score the wall. At round end, each full line sends one tile to the wall. Connected horizontal and vertical runs score more; overflow tiles cost points on the floor.

4. Finish. The game ends after a round in which any player completes a wall row. Bonuses reward completed rows, columns, and full color sets.
