SIX DICE • PUSH YOUR LUCK • HOT DICE

Spark Six

Keep scoring dice, press your luck, and bank before a blank roll burns the whole turn.

Turn0Projected0

Roll six dice to begin.

Default scoring

  • Single 1: 100
  • Single 5: 50
  • Three 1s: 1,000
  • Three 2–6: face × 100
  • 4/5/6 of a kind: 2× / 3× / 4× triple
  • 1–6 straight: 1,500
  • Three exact pairs: 1,500

Use all six scoring dice and the next roll is hot: all six return while your turn score stays alive.

Final round

When someone reaches the target, every other player gets one last turn. Highest total after those turns wins.

Source provenance

Original scoring and match engine. MIT-licensed Farkle projects were used as rules/regression references; no upstream assets or code are included.
