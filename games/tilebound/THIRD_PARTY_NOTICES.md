# Tilebound third-party notices

Tilebound's tile-edge archetypes and the idea of representing tabletop tiles as structured edge/special-feature data are adapted from **DinnerBuffet/TTSCarcassonne**, an MIT-licensed Tabletop Simulator project.

Source: https://github.com/DinnerBuffet/TTSCarcassonne

Referenced source data:

- `tile_data/README.md` sample — starting tile edge pattern.
- `tile_data/base/Card.1d1768.ttslua` — field/city/field/city tile with pennant metadata.
- `tile_data/base/Card.db4061.ttslua` — field/city/field/city variant.
- `tile_data/base/Card.260cca.ttslua` — three-road intersection.
- `tile_data/base/Card.45d85c.ttslua` — road/city/field/road tile.
- `tile_data/base/Card.a8c228.ttslua` — road bend.
- `tile_data/base/Card.e6b112.ttslua` — cloister plus road tile.

Tilebound does **not** copy or redistribute the original game's tile artwork, logos, branded visual design, or rulebook text. It uses original generated SVG graphics and a browser-specific scoring system.
