Tilebound

Rotate and place landscape tiles so every touching edge matches. Roads and cities score more than fields, banners add bonuses, and a fully surrounded cloister scores nine.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Rotate the drawn tile.
  2. Choose a highlighted legal square.
  3. Score every matching touching edge.
  4. Surround all eight spaces around your cloister for +9.
  5. Highest score when the deck ends wins.
