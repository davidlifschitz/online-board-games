    FIELD MANUAL

    How Frontiers works

    1 · Reinforce

    Gain at least 3 armies, plus more for holding territory and complete regions. Deploy them before marching.

    2 · Attack

    Attack adjacent enemy land while leaving one army behind. Commit up to 4 attackers; defenders commit up to 3.

    3 · Clash

    Each committed army rolls a d6. Rolls of 5–6 score hits simultaneously. If nobody hits, the highest roll breaks the deadlock.

    4 · Fortify

    Once per turn, move armies along any connected chain of territories you control. Every territory must remain occupied.

    Frontier cards

    Conquer at least once to draw a Forge, Beacon, Aegis, or rare Prism. Redeem three matching symbols or a mixed trio for escalating reinforcements.

    Victory

    Choose domination, capital ascendancy, an objective mission, or a turn-limited score race before the game.

    Capital Ascendancy requires holding your own capital plus a majority threshold of capitals. Score mode rewards territory, regions, capitals, and fielded armies.
