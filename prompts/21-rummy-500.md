# Rummy 500

Build an open-source browser Rummy 500 game with configurable traditional rules.

## Core gameplay
- 2–6 players.
- Draw from the stock or take a legal portion of the discard pile according to configured rules.
- Meld sets and runs to the table.
- Allow adding cards to existing melds.
- Track positive meld value and negative cards left in hand.
- Play rounds until a configurable target score, commonly 500.

## Modes
- Bots, local pass-and-play, and online multiplayer rooms.

## AI
- Evaluate meld potential, discard-pile pickups, opponent-visible needs, hand liability, and round-ending risk.

## UI/engineering
- Private hand with drag-to-meld or tap controls.
- Shared meld table and vertically visible discard pile history.
- Rules settings for jokers, ace behavior, pickup restrictions, and scoring variants.
- Tests for set/run validation, discard pickup, layoffs, scoring, and round termination.
- Original UI, open-source license, README, Vercel deployment.
