# Secret-government-style social deduction game

Build an original open-source hidden-faction legislative social-deduction game inspired by election-and-policy board games. Do not copy Secret Hitler branding, historical theme, board layout, role names, art, or exact rules text.

## Original concept
Use a fictional space-station council or fantasy republic. Players belong to a public-majority faction, a hidden-minority faction, and one special hidden leader.

## Gameplay
- 5–10 players.
- Rotate an executive role that nominates a deputy.
- Table votes to approve/reject the government.
- Approved leaders draw/discard hidden policy cards through a two-step private process.
- Policy tracks unlock original executive powers.
- Include a special alternate victory condition involving the hidden leader after a threshold.

## Multiplayer
- Online-first with room codes, private roles, synchronized ballots, reconnects, spectators.
- Strict state redaction so clients receive only authorized policies/roles.
