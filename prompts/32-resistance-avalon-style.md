# Resistance/Avalon-style mission deduction game

Build an original open-source hidden-team mission-voting social-deduction game. Create original factions, roles, theme, visuals, and terminology.

## Core gameplay
- 5–10 players split secretly into loyal and infiltrator factions.
- A rotating leader proposes a mission team.
- Everyone votes to approve/reject the team.
- Approved mission participants secretly submit success/sabotage choices according to role permissions.
- Mission results reveal only aggregate outcome.
- First faction to reach its mission-win target wins, with optional endgame role-identification twist.

## Roles/variants
- Base mode with simple factions.
- Advanced roles that alter information at setup or victory conditions, all original.

## Online requirements
- Private role delivery, anonymous mission votes, room codes, reconnect, spectators, host settings.
- No hidden-role or ballot leakage to client DOM/state.

## Tests/UI
- Team-size tables, vote resolution, rejection streaks, mission rules, role knowledge, endgame conditions.
- Strong discussion-oriented lobby/table UI, open-source license, README, Vercel deployment.
